Hearthlight

Hearth, evolvedweb/src/themes/hearthlight.css
Mood
A candle-lit kitchen table: warm, soft and familiar.
Type
Ready when you are. Nunito (rounded) for headings and body; extra-bold headings.
Motion and reward
glow: a warm halo blooms behind the check button and fades. (--ember-reward: glow, 420ms, off under reduced motion)
Optimizes for
Continuity with today’s Hearth and the softest, friendliest first impression.
Risks
Closest to the current app, so the least new. Round cells leave a little less room for the date. Borderless cards rely on a shadow that is faint in dark mode. The honey delete outline sits close to the amber primary, so the farewell card and wording carry it.
Token-driven differences
Round day cells and check buttons (--cell-radius 9999px), no card borders (--border-width 0, --shadow-card carries the edge), 16px radius, one font family. Layout, copy, icons and sizes are identical in every direction; only tokens change.

Fieldnotes

newweb/src/themes/fieldnotes.css
Mood
A pocket notebook on a desk: grounded, quiet, a little literary.
Type
Ready when you are. Fraunces (soft serif) headings; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next body, built for telling similar letters apart.
Motion and reward
fill: ink soaks the box from the bottom up while the row tint sweeps in. (--ember-reward: fill, 440ms, off under reduced motion)
Optimizes for
Readability and low stimulation: a legibility-first body font, flat paper, no shadows, square boxes like a paper tracker.
Risks
Serif headings feel more journal than app. Two font families to ship. Moss green for done may read as a score to some people.
Token-driven differences
Square stamped cells (--cell-radius 3px), 1px ink-blue hairlines, --shadow-card none, 6px radius, blue focus ring separate from the moss primary. Layout, copy, icons and sizes are identical in every direction; only tokens change.

Tidepool

newweb/src/themes/tidepool.css
Mood
Early morning by the water: cool, airy, unhurried.
Type
Ready when you are. Lexend for everything (designed for reading fluency); medium-weight headings with tight tracking.
Motion and reward
rise: the check floats up into place and one small bubble rises and fades. (--ember-reward: rise, 480ms, off under reduced motion)
Optimizes for
Calm and focus: the most spacious density and a cool palette where the flame and the honey notices are the only warm colours.
Risks
Moves away from the warm “ember” feel of the name. More scrolling at 360 px because of the extra space. Teal done and lavender low day rely on their icons for colour-blind users.
Token-driven differences
Spacious density (--space-unit 5px), 18px radius with soft squircle cells (--cell-radius 14px), the slowest, gentlest motion curve. Layout, copy, icons and sizes are identical in every direction; only tokens change.

Marigold

newweb/src/themes/marigold.css
Mood
A sticker sheet on the fridge: playful, confident, high contrast.
Type
Ready when you are. Bricolage Grotesque extra-bold headings with tight tracking; Figtree body.
Motion and reward
fill, fast: the box stamps full in a short, snappy beat. (--ember-reward: fill, 300ms, off under reduced motion)
Optimizes for
Clarity at a glance and personality: ink outlines and a hard offset shadow make every target and state obvious.
Risks
The loudest direction; 2px outlines everywhere can feel busy for sensory-sensitive people. Done is dark ink in light mode, which flips the usual “colour means done”.
Token-driven differences
2px ink borders (--border-width 2px), hard offset shadow (--shadow-card 3px 3px 0), butter secondary, the fastest motion. Layout, copy, icons and sizes are identical in every direction; only tokens change.
Palette
